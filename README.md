# 📢 Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Description

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance data to evaluate the effectiveness of different campaign types across regions. The dashboard provides insights into total spend, revenue generated, return on investment (ROI) and campaign performance, helping businesses optimize their marketing strategies and budget allocation.

## ❗ Business Problem
- Inefficient allocation of marketing budget
- Lack of visibility into campaign performance
- Difficulty in identifying high ROI campaigns
- No centralized system to track marketing effectiveness
  
## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Analyze total spend and revenue across regions
- Evaluate campaign-wise ROI performance
Identify best-performing marketing campaigns
- Compare digital vs traditional marketing effectiveness
- Provide data-driven insights for budget optimization
  
## 🛠 Tech Skills

- Python → Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Pandas, NumPy)
- Power BI → Dashboard & Data Visualization

## 📊 Key Insights
- 📢 Influencer Marketing generated the highest ROI
- 💻 Digital campaigns outperform traditional campaigns in ROI
- 🌍 Regions like Africa and Asia show higher revenue relative to spend
- 📊 Some campaigns have high spend but lower ROI → optimization needed
- 📉 Traditional channels like TV and Radio show comparatively lower efficiency
